Transferable inference reservations

Contested / paid premium unverified

What already exists

Touchmark describes fixed monthly token blocks, provider-specific service terms, and provider-consented relisting before first use on eligible series. Its broader pitch also shows hourly throughput and service metrics. YC lists its Summer 2026 batch and founders with product, trading and engineering experience. Total funding and independent paid resale evidence are unverified. Touchmark’s 4T+ tokens of RFQ volume is a company-reported quoting metric. It provides no settled trade count, repeat buyer rate or price premium for transferability.

Other builders

SF Compute’s September 28 case study names Standard Intelligence as a reseller and Roboflow as a short-term buyer. This is named customer evidence in a vendor publication for physical GPU hours. Architect’s October 7 launch describes a live per-request spot auction with latency, throughput and data-handling filters, alongside its block brokerage. It offers an immediate purchasing substitute. Paid inference volume and the overlap of its compute forwards with assignable inference remain unverified. Tuarā claims matching and settlement but shows indicative pricing and a waitlist. Tokexchange describes a closed pilot. Corgi explicitly says its resale workflow is coming soon. Funding and settled usage for these early attempts were not established in this review. OpenAI’s flexible reserved tier is a strong substitute for internal reallocation.

What a buyer might pay for

Target buyers with large take-or-pay commitments and repeated release, region or model changes. The incremental price ceiling is the probability of stranding capacity times expected recoverable value, adjusted for alternatives and fees. Illustratively, a $100,000 commitment with a 20% chance of stranding $30,000 and 50% net recovery yields $3,000 in expected recovery before friction. Resale liquidity may disappear when many customers move off the same model. An SLA is valuable separately and must be priced separately.

How to test the remaining gap

Start with one approved model and region, one host, two vetted buyers and a provider-approved assignment addendum. Clear by negotiated price and release payment when the host reissues entitlements and acknowledges the remaining balance. Hold service metrics and terms fixed across quotes. Capture latency under load, security eligibility, settlement fees and shortfall remedies. Cross-host substitution needs comparable model fidelity, data restrictions and capacity guarantees. A paid partial transfer is a more useful milestone than a public order book.

Recycled resin offtake and working capital

Contested / one-lane pilot

Where the market already operates

Plastics For Change sells documented recycled material and offers branded cleanups. The Body Shop independently confirms actual use of Indian material in its own program. Circulate Capital’s follow-on in Lucro is funded competition on the capital side, and the investor reports traceability and long-term brand agreements. A broad claim that India lacks trusted routes to international buyers is untenable. The opening is a narrow, repeatable lot finance and offtake service for a specific grade or supplier that incumbents underserve.

What Article 7 actually creates

The EU PPWR sets 35% for the residual other-plastic-packaging category, with separate targets for contact-sensitive packaging and beverage bottles, subject to exemptions. It uses the later of January 1, 2030 and three years after the implementing act enters into force. Article 7 provides conditional third-country routes and mandates calculation, verification and equivalence measures by December 31, 2026. This review did not verify an adopted methodology approving a specific Indian plant or shipment. Treat California and Ontario producer responsibility as separate regimes and avoid assuming an equivalent resin requirement.

How the cash and material move

Use a signed converter specification and purchase order, independent chain-of-custody and batch QA, with settlement against accepted weight and grade. Start by brokering a transaction and let a trade-finance partner take the risk, ideally against an accepted invoice until rejection risk is understood. Underwrite net spread after yield loss, rejects, logistics, duties, FX, audit and financing. Confirm classification and import eligibility for the actual material and lane before committing. Pay collectors on transparent terms and record gross collected weight, sorting yield, saleable tonnes and rejects separately. If buyers value visible cleanup above material compliance, give that a separately priced line and measure it separately.

Falsification

Get three landed quotes including incumbent supply, plus a written buyer price and certifier acceptance for one material specification. Stop if the buyer will not sign, the recycler already gets equivalent terms, or one rejection wipes out the repeat-order economics. A domestic sale can test delivery and financing but cannot validate EU eligibility.

Sweat folate measurement for nutrition studies

Research pilot / clinical utility unknown

Scientific evidence

The April 2026 Nature Communications paper detects B1, B2, B7, B9, B12 and D in sweat and reports a sweat–serum correlation of r=0.849 for folate in its human evaluation. The strongest case is serial folate response after a defined intervention. Correlation does not show individual agreement, diagnostic sensitivity or equivalence across all six vitamins. UC San Diego’s ring adds prototype evidence for sweat vitamin C. Commercial hydration patches establish a sampling workflow for different analytes. Paid adoption for wearable vitamin measurement was not verified.

Commercial experiment

Sell a research measurement service with a lab or CRO, starting with a controlled feeding or supplementation study. Prespecify timing, within-person repeatability, paired agreement and acceptable failed collections. Standardize collection site, sweat rate, induction, skin preparation and calibration. Compare cost per usable curve and decision value with sparse blood sampling plus diaries. Agree on the sponsor decision before building hardware. Clinical or consumer deficiency claims need separate validation and regulatory assessment.

Market structure

A sponsored validation contract can fund a dataset with participant consent and controlled access. This is a research service opportunity. There is insufficient evidence for an exchange in biomarker data or a direct-to-consumer vitamin tracker.
